A little machine. A lot to explore.

Native on your Mac

A Swift app with keyboard, mouse, joystick controls, and Paula audio. Open your ROM and disk images from the Machine menu.

Floppies & hard disks

Load ADF floppy images and HDF hard disks. Build a hard disk from a folder or LHA archive, with configurable Fast RAM.

Open by design

Explore the emulator and its local 68000 core, build from source, or help improve compatibility. The project is actively evolving.

Start with your own collection.

  1. Download the ZIP, unzip it, and move MacMiga.app to Applications.
  2. Open MacMiga and choose your own raw Kickstart ROM image. Kickstart 1.3 and 3.1 have been tested.
  3. Use Machine → Insert Disk (DF0) to select an ADF, or attach an HDF hard disk.
Early release, honest expectations.

Compatibility is a work in progress. No Kickstart ROMs, operating systems, or games are bundled. Supply images you have the right to use.
